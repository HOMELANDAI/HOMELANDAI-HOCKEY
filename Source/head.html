HOW TO PLAY

KEYBOARD + MOUSE

WASD / arrows — skate or move in the crease.
Shift — sprint (uses stamina).
Mouse — place the goal target.
Space / left click — hold to charge; release to shoot. As goalie, press for a timed save.
X / Tap-to-shoot button — instant shot or release a charge.
F — pass. Tab — switch skater.
C — poke check. R — reset drill.
Esc / P — pause and change views.
Right-drag — look around in first person. Q / E — 30-degree snap turn.

GAMEPAD / TOUCH

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, RT shoots/saves, A passes, Y switches, X checks, RB sprints, B pauses.

Touch: use the on-screen direction pad and action buttons. Drag in the scene to place your shot.

IMMERSIVE WEBXR (EXPERIMENTAL)

Trigger: shoot/save/select. Trackpad: skate. Tap trackpad: pass or switch. Hold trackpad for one second: menu. Aim the controller at the net. The in-world MENU button is always available.

Actual Flow button exposure depends on the headset browser. Open the VR menu to see detected controller data. VR and Flow controls have not been device-tested.

Shots always leave the stick when released. In full-length training, complete the rink-length carry for a qualifying attempt; early shots are marked incomplete rather than blocked. Standard Hockey uses five skaters and one goalie per team, three periods, faceoffs and changing ends. This prototype uses simplified rules: no icing, offsides or penalties. First-person locomotion can cause discomfort: start seated, use short sessions, and stop if uncomfortable.
